ABOUT US

Sport tests more than
physical ability.

Mindset Athletics was created around a simple idea: athletes should have a place where the mental side of sport is taken seriously.

Our small team combines psychological support with practical understanding of the sporting environment and the business side behind it. We focus on listening first, then finding useful strategies that fit the individual.
